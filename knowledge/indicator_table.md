# Indicator table (frontend and detail views)

| Card | How measured | Hazard | Key limits (grade) | Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Oxygen and CO2 | Cabin sensors, symptom check-in | Confusion, headache, danger to life | O2 19.5 to 23.1 kPa (A). CO2 1-hour average max 3 mmHg (A). Symptoms from 2.3 mmHg (A). | Fans on, fresh air, check scrubber, report |
| Radiation | Dosimeters, cumulative mSv, dose rate | Cancer risk, storm sickness | Career under 600 mSv (A). Design storm 250 mSv (A). Cosmic rays under 1.3 mSv/day free space (A). | Storm shelter, limit time outside |
| Sleep and fatigue | Wearable, diary, PVT-B reaction test, webcam PERCLOS | Mistakes, poor judgment | 8.5 h scheduled, about 6.1 h real (A). Lapse = 355 ms or more (A). | Protect schedule, limit sleep pills |
| Heart and fitness | HR, HRV, BP, exercise log | Heart, muscle, bone loss | Own baseline. 2.5 h/day exercise (A). Bone loss 1 to 1.5 percent/month (A). Calf muscle down 13 percent in 6 months (A). | Finish exercise plan, check for illness |
| Mental stress | Mood check-in, cognitive test, trend | Low mood, team conflict | No fixed number. Track 14-day trend (A). | Check-in talk, rest, ground support |

Status colours: green normal, yellow watch, red act now, critical emergency. The
colour bands are TEAM choices (see data/demo_data.json).
