| 1 | Solar particle event limit 250 mSv | VERIFIED | NASA-STD-3001 Vol 1 Rev C [V1 4031]. It is a vehicle design requirement for the October 1989 reference storm, not a personal alarm level. |
| 2 | Cosmic ray dose 1.3 and 0.9 mSv/day | VERIFIED | NASA-STD-3001 Vol 1 Rev C [V1 4033], habitats over 60 days. An older 2021 overview said 0.8 for surfaces; use 0.9. |
