# Glossary and unit conversions

- mSv (millisievert): unit of radiation dose that reflects biological effect.
- Dose equivalent: radiation dose adjusted for how harmful each particle type is.
- GCR: galactic cosmic rays, steady radiation from outside the solar system.
- SPE / SEP: solar particle event, a sudden burst of particles from a solar storm.
- ALARA: As Low As Reasonably Achievable, the radiation protection principle.
- ppCO2 / ppO2: partial pressure of CO2 or oxygen, how much of the cabin pressure each gas makes.
- mmHg: millimetres of mercury, a pressure unit. kPa is the metric one.
- SMAC: Spacecraft Maximum Allowable Concentration for air contaminants.
- PVT-B: Psychomotor Vigilance Test, Brief, a 3-minute reaction-time test.
- Lapse: a PVT-B reaction of 355 ms or more.
- PERCLOS: percentage of time the eyes are at least 80 percent closed.
- EAR: eye aspect ratio, a number from face landmarks that drops when an eye closes.
- HRV: heart rate variability, variation in time between heartbeats.
- BMD: bone mineral density.
- ARED: Advanced Resistive Exercise Device on the ISS.
- SANS: Spaceflight Associated Neuro-ocular Syndrome, eye and vision changes.
- IMM: NASA Integrated Medical Model, predicts medical events on missions.
- CMO-DA: Crew Medical Officer Digital Assistant, NASA and Google AI prototype.
- APEX: NASA's suite of tests for psychology in extreme environments.
- OWLT: one-way light time, the one-way communication delay.
- z-score: how many standard deviations a value is from a person's own average.

## Conversions
- 1 mmHg = 0.1333 kPa = about 1,316 ppm at 1 atmosphere (760 mmHg).
- 3 mmHg = 0.40 kPa = about 3,950 ppm. 4 mmHg = 0.53 kPa = about 5,260 ppm.
- 1 mSv per day for 365 days = 365 mSv.
- 600 mSv at 1.84 mSv per day = about 326 days.
